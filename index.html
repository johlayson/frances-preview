Our story

Accessible luxury, everyday glamour

I've always loved fashion and accessories. I was drawn to the elegance and timelessness of traditional jewellery, but I wanted a more accessible way to wear it.

That's why I started FRANCES: pieces that are timeless, elevated and easy to wear, whether you style them the classic way or with jeans and a white tee.
